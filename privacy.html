Privacy Policy

StudyPulse is a personal-use academic reminder application. This policy explains how information, including mobile phone numbers, is handled.

What We Collect

StudyPulse stores the phone number and contact details you enter directly into the app's private settings, solely to send you your own academic reminders.

How We Use Your Information

Your mobile number is used only to deliver text message reminders about assignments, quizzes, and due dates that you configure within the app. Message frequency varies based on your settings, typically up to a few messages per day (e.g., morning and evening summaries) plus occasional weekly summaries.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your wireless carrier for details about your messaging plan.

No Sharing of Mobile Numbers

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or any personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your number is used exclusively to deliver the reminders you configured.

Opting Out

You can stop receiving messages at any time by disabling reminders within the app, or by replying STOP to any message.
